Incident Response Runbook

Severity 1 (full outage): page on-call immediately via PagerDuty, open a incident channel
within 5 minutes, and post a status update every 30 minutes until resolved. A postmortem is
required within 3 business days.

Severity 2 (partial degradation): notify the on-call engineer, no page required outside
business hours. Status updates every 2 hours. Postmortem optional.

All incidents must be logged in the incident tracker with timestamps, impact, and root cause.
