Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may expense meals up to $50/day while traveling for business, and must submit
receipts for any single expense over $25. Reimbursement requests must be filed within 60 days
of the expense date. Alcohol is only reimbursable when part of a client meal, capped at $30
per person. Personal entertainment, gym memberships, and first-class flight upgrades are not
reimbursable.
